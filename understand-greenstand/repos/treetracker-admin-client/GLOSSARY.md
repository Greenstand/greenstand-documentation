## Domains

### Authentication & Access Control

Manages admin user login, session storage, and permission-gated navigation across the Admin Panel. Determines which routes and menu items a signed-in user can access based on their role and organization.

**Terms:** User Session, Permission, Route/Menu Entry, Organization

**Rules:**

- Unauthenticated users are redirected to /login for any private route.
- Route and menu visibility is derived per-user from hasPermission checks, not hardcoded.
- A 'Freetown' feature flag permission gates a subset of experimental routes.

**Flows:**

- **User Login** (`Login.js form submission (POST auth)`): Authenticates an admin user with credentials, stores the session, and redirects into the permission-gated app shell.
- **Permission-Gated Routing** (`App bootstrap / route table construction`): Builds the set of routes and menu items a user is allowed to see, and blocks direct navigation to unauthorized pages.

### Capture Management

Covers verifying, browsing, exporting, and de-duplicating tree-planting 'captures' (photo submissions) collected by the Treetracker mobile app. This is the core review workflow that turns raw field submissions into verified, tokenized tree records.

**Terms:** Capture, Capture Image, Verification Status, Tag, Duplicate Candidate

**Rules:**

- A capture must be approved or rejected before it is tokenized.
- Approve/reject actions support undo via an in-memory action history.
- Filters (date, species, tag, organization) scope both the verify queue and the captures table.
- The matching tool flags visually/geospatially similar captures as likely duplicates before approval.

**Flows:**

- **Verify Captures** (`/verify route (VerifyView)`): Reviews a paginated queue of unverified capture images and approves, rejects, or undoes verification decisions.
- **Browse & Export Captures** (`/captures route (CapturesView)`): Lists already-processed captures in a filterable table and exports the current result set to CSV.
- **Match Duplicate Capture Images** (`/matching route (MatchingToolView)`): Surfaces visually and geospatially similar capture images so reviewers can identify likely duplicate submissions from the same grower before approval.

### Grower Management

Manages the tree planters ('growers') who submit captures: browsing and filtering the grower directory, and viewing/editing individual grower profiles, registrations, and organization assignment.

**Terms:** Grower, Grower Registration, Grower Selfie, Organization Assignment

**Rules:**

- A grower can be reassigned to a different stakeholder organization, including cleared to null.
- Grower selfie photos are deduplicated from the grower's capture history rather than stored separately.

**Flows:**

- **Browse & Filter Growers** (`/growers route (GrowersView)`): Lists growers in a paginated grid with search/filter controls for finding a specific planter.
- **View & Edit Grower Profile** (`Grower detail dialog / GrowerDetail component`): Loads a single grower's profile, registration devices, and photos, and allows editing profile fields including organization assignment.

### Reference Data Management

Maintains the master/reference catalogs used throughout the app: tree species taxonomy and geographic regions (with GeoJSON boundaries). Both are simple CRUD catalogs referenced by captures, growers, and messaging.

**Terms:** Species, Region, Region GeoJSON Boundary

**Rules:**

- Duplicate species entries can be merged ('combined') rather than only deleted.
- Region boundaries are stored and edited as GeoJSON.
- Deleting a species or region requires an explicit confirmation dialog.

**Flows:**

- **Manage Species Catalog** (`/species route (SpeciesView)`): Views, edits, merges, and deletes entries in the tree species catalog.
- **Manage Regions & Boundaries** (`/regions route (RegionsView)`): Views, edits (including GeoJSON boundary upload), and deletes geographic regions used to scope growers and messaging.

### Stakeholder & Organization Management

Manages stakeholder organizations (NGOs, partners, groups) that growers and users belong to, including their parent/child organizational hierarchy and membership lists.

**Terms:** Stakeholder, Stakeholder Group, Parent/Child Relationship, Member Grower/User

**Rules:**

- Stakeholders can be organized in a parent/child hierarchy.
- A stakeholder detail view lists both member growers and member users.
- Adding a stakeholder requires form validation before submission.

**Flows:**

- **Browse & Filter Stakeholders** (`/stakeholders route (StakeholdersView)`): Lists stakeholder organizations in a sortable table with filter controls.
- **Manage Stakeholder Detail & Relationships** (`Stakeholder detail modal`): Views and edits a single stakeholder's profile, parent/child organization hierarchy, and member growers/users.
- **Add New Stakeholder** (`Add stakeholder modal`): Creates a new stakeholder organization via a validated form.

### Messaging & Surveys

Handles direct and broadcast messaging with growers, including announcements and grower surveys, plus visualization of aggregated survey responses.

**Terms:** Message, Announcement, Survey, Survey Response, Inbox Thread

**Rules:**

- Announcements can be broadcast to many growers at once, distinct from a single-recipient message.
- Survey responses are rendered as distinct chat-bubble types from plain sent/received messages.
- Most messaging API calls are scoped by the session organization UUID.

**Flows:**

- **Send Message / Announcement** (`/messaging route (MessagingView)`): Browses the message inbox, composes a new one-to-one message or a broadcast announcement, and sends it to grower(s).
- **Conduct Grower Survey** (`Survey composer within Messaging`): Builds and sends a survey to growers, then visualizes the aggregated responses.

### Earnings & Payments Reporting

Tracks grower earnings from verified/tokenized captures and records payment confirmations, including bulk CSV-based adjustment of earnings and payment logs.

**Terms:** Earning Record, Payment Confirmation, CSV Batch Upload

**Rules:**

- Earnings can be patched individually or in bulk via CSV upload.
- Logging a payment confirmation (id + method) updates the underlying earning record.

**Flows:**

- **Review & Adjust Earnings** (`/earnings route (EarningsView)`): Views paginated grower earnings and adjusts them individually or via bulk CSV upload.
- **Log Grower Payments** (`/payments route (PaymentsView)`): Views payment records with date-range filtering, bulk CSV upload, and logs payment confirmation details against an earning.

### Reporting & Analytics Dashboard

Presents the admin home page with at-a-glance stat cards and date-range-filterable reporting cards summarizing captures, growers, trees, and species across the organization.

**Terms:** Dashboard Stat, Reporting Card, Date Range Filter

**Rules:**

- Dashboard sections and menus are gated by user permissions.
- Reporting cards show a top-N breakdown alongside the headline stat, with a 'see more' modal for detail.

**Flows:**

- **View Dashboard Overview** (`/ route (Home)`): Renders the home page's stat cards and reporting cards for a selected date range, summarizing captures, growers, trees, and species activity.
